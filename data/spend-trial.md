# Spend Trial

I started building Spend Trial for my advanced database management systems course. The idea came from something I wanted to fix for myself. I tend to spend more than I plan to, and I find it hard to keep track of where my money goes when it's spread across several accounts. I wanted one place to see what I'm spending and set a budget I can actually follow.

There are already apps for this, but I wanted to build my own because I'm interested in mobile app development. A course project gave me a reason to work on an app I'd actually want to use. It also gives me something for my portfolio that I can explain from experience, starting with why I wanted it in the first place.

One design question I'm thinking about is how to bring spending from multiple accounts together in a way that's easy to follow. If I'm checking whether I still have room in my budget, I want to see the overall picture in one place. That's the part of the idea I care about most. I also want the app to help me notice when I'm getting close to my budget so I can adjust my spending.

I've made an initial version of Spend Trial. In the future, I'd like to explore adding cards and making payments from inside the app. The idea is to bring payments, spending history, and budgeting closer together. Those are features I'm considering for later versions. For now, my goal is to make something that helps me understand where my money goes while giving me more practice building mobile apps.
